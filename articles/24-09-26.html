If I was more of a loner, and received much less joy from my neighbours, it would all be a non-starter. It is remarkable that in passing moments I am able to overcome the torturous psychic assault that has been reaching for my neck since before I first walked. I breathe strangely. My legs hardly function. They are intruded by splinters and sticks and consequently my knees buckle towards the ground. My hands are sooty, and I have dreams of my teeth falling apart, out, one by one or all at once. My fingers are crooked like the branches of a hollowed tree that has no more than two winters left in it.

My neighbours are dear to me; they send me sweet letters.
