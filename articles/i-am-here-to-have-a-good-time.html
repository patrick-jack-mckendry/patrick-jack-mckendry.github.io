I am Here (To Have a Good Time)

I am walking between two bridges Hauling a carcass And As I drop him over the ledge His legs drooping downward dog His sack scratching at the sog His feet finding friends in frogs I find myself sitting on a log And wiggle my toes. I am wearing a straw hat. I have a child’s frame. My eyes are green and blue. My teeth are big and yellow. I like to make them glow. Soon, I lose myself to the well of time And seek sorry songs to infuse with rhyme But for now I walk on cobbled streets With ankled feet And come upon the door of my brother. And as he binds brains to his chest He lets my tired headache rest And in the way that he knows best He waters my crocs. Lights a fire. Moves his body hard against my skull. And whispers in my ear…

A fish that talks and a dog that walks is no good for nobody I am here to have a good time, A really good time, My lovely, So we will grab drums and go hum round the fire now, Quickly now, Faster now, Always faster now, Fast, Fast, Humdrum fast, Lungsong fast, Fast, Fast.

And these words I repeat to my daughter.
